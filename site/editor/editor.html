    USB · FM-1

    Help and licences

    Save this HTML file to use it offline. For USB transfer, use Chrome or Edge and allow MIDI SysEx. The editor writes to the device only when you press a write or send button. Reading the bank replaces your working bank.

    On the FM-1, use HOME to reach PHRASE and SELECT to pick a phrase. Play the keys to sing its syllables or press REC to place it in the current row's VOCAL pattern. Stop the FM-1 before writing phrases. After sending a song, press REC within 10 seconds to accept it, then SAVE to store it.

    For Japanese, separate romaji with spaces or hyphens. Add :4 for a length of four sixteenths, use _ to hold, and / or | for a rest. MIDI import uses the first tempo and lays out notes consecutively with lengths rounded to sixteenths.
